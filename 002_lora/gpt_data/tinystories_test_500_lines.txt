Every day, Finn gave it some water and put it beside the sunny window.
For a while nothing happened, and Finn wondered if the seed was asleep.
Then a small green shoot appeared, with two leaves as tiny as buttons.
Finn showed the plant to Grandma and learned that growing takes time.
